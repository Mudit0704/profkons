Consulting services

Six areas of business consulting

Each engagement starts with a diagnosis of your business and ends with changes your team can run without us.

How we work

Every engagement follows the same five stages.

  1. 01Diagnose

    Study the numbers, processes and people to find the root cause.

  2. 02Redesign

    Design practical processes, roles and controls to fix it.

  3. 03Implement

    Put the changes in place with owners and timelines.

  4. 04Measure

    Track results against agreed KPIs and correct course.

  5. 05Sustain

    Hand over systems and reviews so results last after we leave.

  1. 01Business Transformation
  2. 02Organisational Development
  3. 03Operational Excellence
  4. 04Profitability & Financial Discipline

01

Business Transformation

The problem
The business runs on informal ways of working, or an ERP has gone live without fixing the processes underneath.
What it costs you
Work depends on a few individuals, the same errors repeat, and the ERP adds cost without adding control.
How ProfKons helps
We map how work actually flows, redesign the process, then align your operating model and ERP to it.

A bad process digitised is still a bad process.

Focus areas

  • Business diagnosis
  • Process redesign
  • Operating model design
  • ERP & digital process alignment
  • Management systems
02

Organisational Development

The problem
Every decision goes through the promoter. Roles overlap and accountability is unclear.
What it costs you
Growth is capped by one person’s time, good people leave, and work falls between teams.
How ProfKons helps
We design the organisation structure, define roles and KRAs, write SOPs, and set up a performance management system.

Focus areas

  • Organisation structure
  • Role clarity & KRAs
  • SOPs & work instructions
  • SLAs between teams
  • Performance management system (PMS)
  • HRMS
  • Leadership development
03

Operational Excellence

The problem
Capacity sits idle, output varies and shop-floor costs keep rising.
What it costs you
Higher unit costs, missed delivery dates and margin lost to waste and rework.
How ProfKons helps
We improve production planning, productivity, procurement and quality systems, and put operational controls in place.

Focus areas

  • Process improvement
  • Productivity & capacity utilisation
  • Production planning
  • Waste reduction
  • Procurement & vendor development
  • Quality systems
  • Cost optimisation
  • Operational controls
04

Profitability & Financial Discipline

The problem
Sales grow but profit doesn’t. Budgets exist but aren’t used, and cash is always tight.
What it costs you
Margin leaks unseen, working capital is funded by expensive borrowing, and decisions are made without numbers.
How ProfKons helps
We analyse costs and margins, build the annual operating plan and budget, and set up MIS and dashboards reviewed every month.

Focus areas

  • Cost & margin analysis
  • Annual operating plan & budgeting
  • Budgetary controls
  • Working capital management
  • Cash flow planning
  • MIS & management dashboards
05

Strategy & Execution

The problem
Plans are made every year, but targets slip and priorities keep changing.
What it costs you
Effort spreads thin, investments don’t pay back, and the team stops trusting the plan.
How ProfKons helps
We set clear priorities, turn them into an execution roadmap with KPIs and KRAs, and run management reviews until the habit sticks.

Focus areas

  • Business strategy
  • Annual business planning
  • Growth planning & market evaluation
  • Execution roadmaps
  • KPI/KRA framework
  • Management review meetings
06

Governance & Management Advisory

The problem
Controls and decision rules are informal and depend on trust.
What it costs you
Risk of fraud, errors and compliance gaps grows with the business, and lenders, investors and boards notice.
How ProfKons helps
As an IICA certified independent director, Abhay helps set up internal controls, delegation of authority and board-level oversight.

Focus areas

  • Corporate governance
  • Internal controls
  • Delegation of authority
  • Decision-making frameworks
  • Risk review
  • Board advisory
