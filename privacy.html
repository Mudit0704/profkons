Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains how ProfKons handles information when you visit this website or contact us.

Information you share with us

When you write to us, by email, phone or through the contact form, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, company, designation, email address, phone number and message. The contact form opens your own email app; the website itself does not store what you type.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and understand your business requirement.
  • To discuss and deliver any engagement you choose to take forward with us.

We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

Confidentiality

Business information shared with us during conversations or engagements is treated as confidential and used only for the purpose it was shared.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set cookies or use analytics tracking.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete the information you have shared by writing to inquiry@profkons.com.
